# asi_fusa_tools

One of the [ASI FuSa tools](https://github.com/c00289/c00289/blob/main/README.md) by Roy Mendoza; the master README there lists every sibling.

The charter of the ASI FuSa Tools: eight sibling repositories that make up the functional-safety
tooling for the Wheel Loader programme. This repo holds one document and no code.

- The document: [ASI_FUSA_TOOLS.md](ASI_FUSA_TOOLS.md), the ontology, the house rules, the policies
  (Jira and Confluence read only, controlled documents private, the sprint rule, the privacy wording, exports
  filling the Word original, Dewey experimental and Kletz the home of work products and STPA), the
  conventions every sibling tests against (the family lock, the family palette, the JIRA Live
  Data window), the version ladder, and the index of every plan.
- To propose a change: write a plan under `docs/` in the repo the change belongs to (or under
  `docs/` here when it changes the charter itself), link it from the plans index, and record the
  revision in `CHANGELOG.md` with its date. Revisions are dated, never versioned.
